# DOGGYWOOD DESIGN SYSTEM

## 1. Brand personality

Doggywood should feel:

Famous

Playful

Premium

Warm

Energetic

Easy to understand

The visual reference is not a children's cartoon.

The visual reference is a modern entertainment platform where the celebrities happen to be dogs.

## 2. Two visual states

### 2.1 Launch State

The launch landing page is bright, light, clean, playful, and Hollywood inspired.

It should feel fun and aspirational without looking childish or visually crowded.

This state must not use fake activity or a social feed.

### 2.2 Future Social State

After real approved content exists, Doggywood can become a social discovery experience.

Video playback surfaces may use a dark media canvas because that is the video stage, not a marketing-page background.

The launch landing page still must not use black backgrounds or dark theme sections.

## 3. Brand promise

Hollywood has stars. Doggywood has dogs.

## 8. Typography

Use Inter for interface text to maintain consistency with the LoveDecision technical and design approach.

Display headlines should use a large bold Inter treatment before introducing additional brand fonts.

This avoids blocking early development on font licensing and loading.

## 9. Logo direction

Primary wordmark:

DOGGYWOOD

The word should feel cinematic without copying the Hollywood wordmark.

Possible logo device:

A single five point star integrated into one letter or placed above the word.

That star is decorative Hollywood branding.

It is not a contest vote.

Do not use another company's protected logo treatment.

## 10. Core interface shapes

Launch cards:

White or warm cream

Thin border

Moderate radius

Generous padding

Flat icons

No fake vote counts

Future video cards:

Rounded corners

Dark video canvas only for the media surface

Simple overlays

High contrast actions

Profile cards:

Warm white or white

Thin border

Moderate radius

Clear dog portrait

Official contest leaderboard cards:

Rank number is dominant

Dog image

Dog name

Valid vote count

Do not show average star rating as official standing.

## 11. Official voting control

The official voting control is not a five star rating widget.

The official action is:

VOTE FOR THIS DOG

The control should be a clear button or equivalent explicit action.

After voting:

Show that the user voted.

Show the current valid vote count.

Do not imply that decorative stars are the contest score.

Star graphics may appear as Hollywood decoration elsewhere on the page.

## 12. Button hierarchy

Primary:

Light coral or flat gold background

Dark text or white text only when contrast is accessible

No gradient

Examples:

Enter Doggywood

Submit My Premiere

VOTE FOR THIS DOG

Secondary:

Midnight or coral border

Midnight text

Light background

Examples:

Learn About Verify.Dog

View Rules

Tertiary:

Text link with clear focus state

Do not use gradient buttons.

## 16. Photography

Dogs should look real and emotionally expressive.

Prefer:

Portrait photography

Natural environments

Service animal lifestyle images

Owner and dog relationships

Dogs looking directly toward camera

Avoid:

Overly staged stock photography

Artificial fantasy dogs

Visuals that imply celebrities endorse Doggywood

Fake contestant portraits presented as live entries

## 18. Accessibility

Maintain strong color contrast.

Do not rely on color alone to communicate voted state.

All controls require keyboard focus.

Videos need captions where speech is present.

Provide clear text labels for icon controls.

Respect reduced motion preferences.

Provide paused state and user controlled playback where required.

## 19. Responsive priority

Design mobile first because:

Most submissions will come from phones.

Most shared traffic will arrive on phones.

Vertical video is naturally mobile oriented.

Desktop should feel intentionally designed, not stretched from mobile.

On mobile (max-width 1023px), stacked page and section content must follow this presentation order:

1. Headline (eyebrow may sit immediately above it)
2. Image
3. Body / text content
4. CTA button

Skip any slot that does not exist. Desktop may keep overlay or two-column layouts. Source order should match the mobile visual order.

After a CTA, keep 32px of space before the section bottom. Do not sit a button flush on the next section.
